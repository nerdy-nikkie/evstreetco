About

A contractor, not a charging network

Owning an EV in Chicago means a lot of guesswork about where to charge. Public stations fill up, late-night charging is a gamble, and without a garage the options are few. We started EV Street Co to change that.

Our model is simple. The property owner owns the charging station at the curb, sets their own price, and earns every time someone plugs in. We install it, keep the payments running, and take a dollar a session plus ten percent. That is the entire business.

We are not a charging network. We do not want to own equipment on your property or lock you into a ten-year site agreement. The station is yours. If you sell the building, it goes with it.

What we will not do

Upgrade your service quietly

If your panel is full, we tell you and quote it as separate work. You decide. No surprise line items after the fact.

Take a deposit on a property that is wrong for this

No curb frontage, no parking area, or no spare panel capacity. We would rather say so on the first call.

Do informal wiring

Every mains connection is permitted with the city and inspected. There is no version of this where we skip that.

Lock you in

No contract, no monthly fee, and no term. If you want to stop using our payments, the station is still yours.

Find out what your curb is worth

Send us your address and we will take a look at your panel, your curb, and any rebates you may qualify for. If it turns out not to be a good fit, we will tell you honestly. 🙂
