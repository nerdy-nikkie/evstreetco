Chicago is short on places to charge

Chicago EV drivers lack easy access to charging. Unless you have a garage, you are planning your week around finding a plug. Meanwhile you have curb frontage, an electrical panel, and no way to connect the two.

Not enough stations

There are a limited number of public charging stations across the Chicagoland area.

Winter makes it worse

Cold weather cuts range, and a car left unplugged overnight starts the morning with less of it.

Your curb is already there

That is where property owners come in, and where the extra income comes from.

From your address to a paying station

Three steps. You see the full price after step one, and nothing gets ordered or dug until you approve it.

Step 1: Send us the address

We check your panel, the curb, and your block, then quote the install and any rebate you qualify for.

Step 2: We build it

Licensed electricians trench the run, set the conduit and disconnect, and mount the station. Permitted with the city and inspected.

Step 3: You get paid

You set the hourly rate. Our owner app adjusts your pricing for peak utility rates. Drivers tap a card, and the money lands in your account.*

What you provide

  • Curb frontage or a parking area at a property you own
  • Access to 240-volt service
  • About 50 amps of spare capacity in your panel
  • Permission to run conduit and set the pedestal

We verify all of it on site before anything is signed or ordered. If your panel is full, we tell you before you spend money, not after.

*Payout timing. Payout timing is still being finalized. We will confirm your payout schedule in writing, in your service agreement, before your station goes live.

What you own when we leave

The hardware is yours. The rate is yours. The revenue is yours after upkeep. We run the payments and keep the network up.

You own the hardware

The station is yours the day it is installed. No lease, no revenue share on the equipment, and no company that can switch it off.

You set the price

Pick your hourly rate through the app and change it whenever you want. Free for tenants, market rate for the street, or shut off overnight. Your call.

Tap and go

Drivers tap a card at the station. Nothing for them to download, no account to create, no membership. First-time drivers charge on the first try.

We handle the money

We run the transaction and pay out your share. No merchant account to open, no chargebacks to fight, no reconciling. $1 per session + 10%.

Permitted and inspected

Every mains connection is done by our licensed Chicago electrician, permitted with the city and inspected.

Level 2 Charging

240 volts at 50A (9.6 kW)* through a J1772 connector. That covers every non-Tesla EV. Tesla drivers use their own adapter.

*Charging speed disclaimer. Delivered kW depends on a number of factors, including but not limited to what your panel and breaker can handle, the length of the run, the ambient temperature, and what your vehicle accepts. Actual charging speed may vary.

Why we are building this

I run a contracting business in Chicago, and over the past few years of owning an EV in the city, I ran into the same problem again and again: there was nowhere easy to charge. I'd either head to an overcrowded charging station, plan my night around charging at 1 a.m. when no one else was around, or white-knuckle it home hoping I wouldn't run out of charge. The charging infrastructure just wasn't there, and I quickly learned I wasn't the only one feeling it. If you didn't have a garage, you were largely out of luck.

That's why we started building a different model: one where the property owner owns the charging station at the curb, sets their own price, and gets paid every time someone plugs in.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

What does it cost?

Most curbside installs run $4,000 to $5,500, covering the station, trenching, conduit, electrical, permit, and inspection. A panel upgrade costs more. We quote the exact number after we look at your panel and your curb.

Do I own it?

Yes. The station is yours the day it is installed. Sell the building with it, change the rate, or shut it off. We do not lease equipment and we take no cut of the hardware.

What do you charge me?

One dollar per charging session plus ten percent of what that session earned. Nothing monthly, nothing on the hardware, no contract. Your electricity and upkeep come out of your side.

Who sets the price drivers pay?

You do. Pick an hourly rate in the app and change it whenever you want. Market rate for the street, free for tenants, or off overnight. We do not set or approve your pricing.

How do drivers pay?

They tap a card at the station. No app, no account, and no membership on the driver side. We run the transaction and pass your share on to you.

What do I need to qualify?

Curb frontage or a parking area at your property, access to 240-volt service, and about 50 amps of spare capacity in your panel. We verify all three on site before anything is signed or ordered.

What if my panel is full?

Then we quote a panel upgrade as separate work and you decide. Feel free to contact us and we can discuss further options.

Who is this not for?

Renters who do not own the electrical service, and properties with no curb frontage or parking area. Feel free to add your info and we can discuss if you are unsure.
